Spotting Errors (Subject-Verb Agreement)

100 Questions
⚡ Quick Shortcuts
  • A singular subject takes a singular verb (adds -s/-es); a plural subject takes the plain form.
  • Words like 'each', 'every', 'neither', 'everybody' are treated as singular.
  • Ignore words/phrases between the subject and verb — they don't change the agreement rule.
Q1Find the grammatical error in the sentence: "The doctor run in the park."
  1. Subject 'The doctor' is singular, so the verb must take -s/-es.
  2. Correct verb form: 'runs' instead of 'run'.
Answer: 'run' should be 'runs'
Q2Find the grammatical error in the sentence: "The teachers speaks fruits regularly."
  1. Subject 'The teachers' is plural, so the verb must not take -s/-es.
  2. Correct verb form: 'speak' instead of 'speaks'.
Answer: 'speaks' should be 'speak'
